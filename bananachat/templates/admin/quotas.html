How limits work

  • Request rate – how often an account may send requests, e.g. “60 per minute” and “1,000 per day”. Every API key, the playground, chat and agents of one account share it.
  • 5-hour tokens – a window opens with an account's first request and lasts 5 hours; when it ends, the allowance is full again. Optional slow tokens keep people going afterwards, behind everyone else in the queue.
  • Weekly tokens (off by default) – a rolling week that starts with the first request after the previous week ended. When used up, requests wait until the week ends.
  • Models count by their weight (a heavy model ×3 uses the allowance three times as fast) and can have their own limits: see the Models tab.

An account's limit is, in order: its custom limit (set on its page or by an approved request), else the policy below (times its tier when tiers are on) → the dynamic adjustment → the music bonus → grants. {{ custom_count }} account(s) have custom limits; {{ grant_count }} grant(s) are active.
