How pool capacity works

Each account handles one request at a time. A 5-hour budget is required; a blank or zero budget cannot serve requests. Weekly budgets are optional. Higher priority accounts are tried first.

Local budgets count usage recorded by this server; they are estimates, not the provider's billing units. Provider reports expire after 15 minutes or their reported reset time. Expired reports require a fresh observation before the account can answer again.

Each model has its own limits: Fable and Opus are strictest, then Sonnet, then Haiku. Remaining provider capacity reduces these limits, alongside demand adjustment. Claude models do not count toward service token budgets by default; this can be changed in the model's limits.

Another account is tried only before any text or reasoning is emitted. Model fallback follows your Services settings.
