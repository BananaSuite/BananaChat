How pool capacity works{{ icon("chevron-down") }}

Each account answers one request at a time. A 5-hour token budget is required; a blank or zero budget cannot serve requests. Weekly budgets are optional. Higher priority accounts are tried first.

Local budgets count usage recorded by this server; they are estimates, not the provider’s billing units. Provider reports expire after 15 minutes or at their reported reset. An expired report needs a fresh observation before the account can answer again.

Allowance caps keep part of each subscription for its owner: an account stops once it has used that share of the provider’s own 5-hour or weekly allowance, or of one model family’s allowance. A capped account answers only while a fresh provider report shows its usage.

Each model family starts with strict limits per 5 hours: {% for family, policy in strictness.items() %}{{ families[family] }} {{ policy.window_tokens | compact }}{{ " tokens" if loop.last else ", " }}{% endfor %}. Remaining provider capacity reduces them, alongside demand adjustment. {{ setup.name }} models do not count towards service token budgets by default; this can be changed in the model’s limits.

Another account is tried only before any text or reasoning is emitted. Model fallback follows your limit settings.
