How it works

  • Levels, lowest first: Off, Low, Medium, High, Max. Each model offers the levels it supports; models that can only switch thinking on count as Medium.
  • Everyone may use levels up to the default (Medium unless you change it, globally or per model). Higher levels are locked until unlocked: unlocking a level unlocks every level below it.
  • People unlock more by asking (a request on their account page) or automatically after sustained use of a model. You can also set levels for one account on its limits page.
  • Locked levels show a lock in the chat composer with a “Request access” link. The API answers 403 reasoning_effort_locked; agents use at most the unlocked level.
