How the pool works{{ icon("chevron-down") }}
  • Each account answers one request at a time. Higher priority accounts go first.
  • Every account needs a 5-hour token budget. Budgets are counted on this server and are estimates.
  • Provider reports expire after 15 minutes. An allowance cap stops an account at a share of the provider's allowance and needs a fresh report.
  • Strict limits per 5 hours for each model family: {% for family, policy in strictness.items() %}{{ families[family] }} {{ policy.window_tokens | compact }}{{ " tokens" if loop.last else ", " }}{% endfor %}.
  • Another account is tried only before any text is sent. Model fallback follows the limit settings.
